# 2nd SpatialData Hackathon: Frameworks, Formats and Interoperability

2nd SpatialData hackathon was an in-person event organized by the **SpatialData team (scverse)** and **Bioconductor** that 
brought together expertise from different fields, including methods developers of a variety of tools for bioimaging, 
single-cell and spatial omics.

## Venue

 - Hotel Garden Inn Padova City Centre
 - 14-16 April, 2026, Padua, Italy.

## Objective and Teams

The purpose is to explore new directions to advance the field of spatial omics. By leveraging multiple programming languages, 
including Python, R, and JavaScript, the event focuses on four central hackathon tracks:

 - R interoperability for SpatialData and OME-ZARR
 - Accessibility, maintainability and performance of spatial omics visualization tools 
 - Design modernization for the SpatialData framework
 - File formats and transformations (NGFF)
